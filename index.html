Wealth & Health of Nations

55 countries. Left–right: income. Up–down: life expectancy. Depth: years of schooling. Colour: region. Size: population. Point at any country to read it; point at a region in the legend to show or hide it; grab empty space and twist to spin the world. Desktop: drag to orbit, scroll to zoom.
